The homepage and the rest of your site answer different questions. Choose a
portfolio to introduce your work, a blog to share your writing, or docs to explain
your project.

## Compose the pieces

A portfolio homepage can link to the same blog articles. A blog homepage can sit
beside a documentation section. Changing the homepage leaves article URLs and
content in place.

## Start small

Enable the sections you need, then add links through `params.customMenus`.
The docs layout is an opt-in modern surface. This initial blog layout retains
the existing article and archive styling.
