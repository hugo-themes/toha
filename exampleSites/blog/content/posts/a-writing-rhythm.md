Save a question during the week. Write down what you tried, what surprised you,
and what you would do next. A useful post does not need to cover an entire topic.

## Leave a trail

Use tags to connect related ideas and an RSS feed to let readers follow along.
On a blog-first site, the homepage feed follows the configured blog section.
